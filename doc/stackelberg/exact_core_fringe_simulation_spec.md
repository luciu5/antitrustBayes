# Deterministic exact core-fringe simulation contract

Status: implementation specification, 2026-09-15. The equations below are
already established by the Stackelberg math audit and the exact ordered-k Stan
oracle. This note specifies deterministic ownership and cost counterfactuals;
it introduces no smooth roles.

## Ownership

`coordination` owns two deterministic classes, `CoreFringeLogit` and
`CoreFringeCES`, behind one public `core_fringe()` constructor and one
`core_fringe_simulate()` counterfactual helper. A character `conduct` slot
is either `"bertrand"` or `"cournot"`. User-facing `corePre` and
`corePost` arguments contain firm IDs. Ownership remains product-level in
`ownerPre` and `ownerPost`; every product of a core firm receives the same
firm-level strategic FOC. A fringe product receives the exact product-level
MonCom FOC.

The constructor accepts observed prices, unconditional product shares,
optional observed margins, ownership, demand choice, conduct, inside size,
outside price, cost changes, subset, starts, labels, and solver/calibration
controls parallel to `stackelberg()`. It calibrates demand from all usable
margin moments unless alpha/gamma is supplied, recovers baseline costs by
inverting the hard core-fringe FOCs, and reproduces observed prices to
tolerance.

The hard endpoints are statements about the structural equations at fixed
demand parameters. At `k=0`, Logit still uses the required all-moment WLS
criterion in proportional margins and CES uses the required log
inverse-margin distance. These newer calibration objectives need not return
the same demand estimate as the older `antitrust::moncom.*` one-step
calibrators when observed margins are mutually inconsistent. With fixed
alpha/gamma, or margins generated by the model, the `k=0` economics and
recovered equilibrium coincide exactly with MonCom.

Post-merger roles are explicit. If ownership changes, a missing `corePost`
is an error in the deterministic constructor. The Bayesian bridge may apply a
documented default outside this constructor: a merged post owner is core when
any constituent pre owner was core. Callers can override that rule.

## Hard-role margin equations

For Logit, use unconditional firm quantity share S_f and outside quantity share
s0. Let alpha>0 be the absolute price coefficient. Every product of a strategic
firm has common dollar markup h_f/alpha:

* Bertrand core: h_f=1/(1-S_f).
* Cournot core: h_f=1+S_f/s0.
* Fringe: h=1.

Output-market proportional margins are h/(alpha*p). Input-market markdowns
use the signed package convention already implemented by `StackelbergLogit`;
the absolute wedge is h/alpha and cost/value recovery changes orientation.

For CES output demand, use unconditional firm revenue share R_f, outside
revenue share R0, gamma>1, h=gamma-1, H=1+h*R0, and
b=h/(gamma*H):

* Bertrand core Lerner margin: 1/(gamma-h*R_f).
* Cournot core Lerner margin: 1/gamma+b*R_f.
* Fringe Lerner margin: 1/gamma.

The outside price and expenditure normalization must match `antitrust::CES`
and `StackelbergCES` exactly. CES input markets remain unsupported.

## Equilibrium and counterfactuals

For each trial state, recompute demand and aggregate active product shares once
per owner. Solve the simultaneous hard-role FOCs for all active products. This
is BF/CF, not a sequential leader game: core firms are ordinary multiproduct
Bertrand/Cournot strategists and fringe products are atomistic MonCom firms.

The object supports ownership-only, cost-only, and combined shocks, plus exits
through `subset`. Baseline costs are structural and are not reset from noisy
observed margins. Diagnostics report implied versus observed margins, baseline
price error, maximum core FOC residual, maximum fringe FOC residual, active
roles, and solver status.

Required nests are exact:

* k=0 equals all-product MonCom.
* k=N equals ordinary multiproduct Bertrand or Cournot under the corresponding
  demand system.

## Bayesian bridge

For an exact-fringe posterior draw, antitrustBayes selects one supported k or
accepts an explicit fixed k, maps the stored firm-market ranks to firm IDs, and
constructs the coordination core-fringe object. It must never recompute ranking
from a selected year. Absent firms retain their full-market rank; the active
core is the intersection of the fixed top-k IDs and owners present in that
simulation unit.

The same selected market-wide k is cached across all years and pre/post states.
The default post-merger constituent-union rule is recorded in simulation
metadata. Generated covariates and observation order are untouched.

`role_mapping="mixture"` is Monte Carlo integration: one k is sampled from
P(k|D,theta) for each posterior theta draw. Exact probability-weighted outcome
means require solving every supported k for the same theta draw and attaching
the component probabilities. They are summaries over exact games and never
construct a fractional-role game.

## Verification

Test Logit/CES crossed with Bertrand/Cournot, k=0, k=1, several core firms, and
k=N; single- and multiproduct firms; core and fringe multiproduct ownership;
ties and unsorted rows; missing margins; exits; ownership, cost, and combined
shocks; baseline reproduction; core/fringe FOC residuals; and equality to
registered MonCom/simultaneous models in the boundary states. Bridge tests must
include a panel whose period-specific ordering changes, proving that fixed
market ranks and the same k are used in every year.

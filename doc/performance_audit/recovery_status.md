# Execution-environment recovery

The execution environment became unavailable during a filesystem/status call
on September 17, 2026. After execution resumed, the prior `/tmp` working copies,
native libraries and agent sessions were absent. Workspace source changes,
compact evidence, ignored fit archives and saved review patches survived.

## Evidence that survives

* Committed parallel-chain and selective-extraction changes and benchmarks.
* Public baseline-consumer source, 27 paired 200-draw policy comparisons
  and temporary current-refactor suite results.
* Matched-compiler Helmert probes, full-vector and eighteen independent panel
  comparisons, raw schemas and installed code-digest metadata.
* Approved saved-fit compatibility helper, its tests, forty normalized
  saved-row checks and one hundred original-kernel archived-row checks.
* Durable lifecycle, Helmert and unapproved serial-vectorization source patches.
  Large native binaries remain excluded from Git.

## Incomplete evidence

The latest paired vectorization HMC fits and their posterior arrays were saved
only in `/tmp`. A filesystem listing showed both arms' four fit files and
summary files before the reset, but candidate summaries and posterior arrays
were not retrieved or copied into the workspace. Their existence is not a
verified before/after sampler result. No vectorization HMC speedup,
posterior-equivalence finding or acceptance decision is established by them.

Four scalar-arm summary rows were retrieved before the reset. Rounded partial
telemetry is retained in `interrupted_hmc_scalar_telemetry.csv`. The original
runner suppressed warning text and used legacy effective-sample metrics across
all outputs; those deficiencies require explicit reporting. Structural
bulk/tail ESS, posterior comparisons and warning text cannot be reconstructed
from that summary. The equal-share fixture is an artificial stress case, not
a recovery DGP. These rows are not an acceptance gate.

The bridge implementation's first/middle/last context-failure regression was
not completed with retained evidence. Root review found that assigning a NULL
context with double brackets deletes its list entry. The candidate remains
unintegrated pending a list-preserving repair, tests and fresh review.

## Recovery policy

Luna workers are reconstructing isolated dependencies/native builds and the
bridge repair from durable source snapshots. New executable scripts and
compact results must be saved in the workspace before long runs; heavy native
objects and fit arrays use the ignored workspace recovery directory. Rebuilt
comparisons will be labeled as new executions. Priors, economic equations,
likelihoods, role support, pooling and numerical tolerances remain unchanged.

Actual antitrust/refactor and trade/refactor worktrees remain untouched because
another session may be modifying them. Lifecycle patches are temporary review
artifacts, not an authorization to overwrite that session's work.

The seventy pure R migration-policy checks have been rerun successfully in the
fresh environment without the missing native Bayes installation. The recovery
runner is `recovery/run_compatibility_unit.R`. This does not rerun native saved-row
target checks or establish any sampler improvement.

# Market/outside-share rotation: frozen benchmark protocol

Frozen before new HMC execution, 2026-09-16. Mathematical target/gradient gates
must pass first. Do not change priors or settings after observing fit results.

## Primary paired experiment

Use the archived two-market/two-period Logit Bertrand noisy-share fixture
generated by `cga_ladder_fixtures()$multi_market` in
`doc/collapsed_gaussian_benchmark.R`. It has 96 observations and 48 firm groups.
The archived collapsed Gaussian fit had ten divergences and approximately
-0.997 economic intercept/outside-logit correlation. That fit is retained as
historical context, not treated as an independent successful replication.

Run two legacy/rotated pairs on this same frozen fixture, with Stan seeds
271828 and 314159. For each chain, freeze the archived legacy fit's initial
constrained state and map it to rotated coordinates using the approved
transformation. Use the same economic initial states within each pair. The
two seeds change sampler randomness, not the data, priors, or starts.

Both modes use Gaussian shares, collapsed quality, Gaussian margins,
simultaneous Bertrand, output market, baseline prior manifest, rho off,
common residual scales, common alpha, unpooled market-year outside shares,
fixed df5, no HMT. Explicitly retain the original pooling flags; this test does
not substitute automatic market pooling.

Per fit: four chains, 1,000 iterations, 500 warmup, adapt_delta 0.95,
max_treedepth 13, diagonal Euclidean metric, one chain process at a time.
Record complete warnings and sampler artifacts. Matched starts do not imply
that random trajectories or the historical divergence count repeat exactly.

Report elapsed and native Stan time; divergence/depth counts; chain BFMI;
maximum rank R-hat; minimum bulk/tail ESS; structural ESS/second; step size;
posterior variance-component correlations; reconstructed economic
intercept/outside-share correlations; and correlations of sampling
coordinates. Economic correlations may remain high under an exact rotation;
the question is whether the sampler's coordinates and efficiency improve.

A usable fit has no post-warmup divergences or depth hits, BFMI >= 0.3,
maximum rank R-hat <= 1.01, and minimum bulk/tail ESS >= 100. Preserve every
failure and report which condition failed. Empirical posterior, bridge, and
policy comparisons require usable fits; a failed legacy fit cannot establish
posterior equality merely because its posterior means look close.

## Conditional scaling experiment

Proceed only if both primary rotated replications meet the gate. Use a
controlled two-period, six-firm-per-market noisy synthetic design at
M = 10, 100, 500, 2000, hence N = 12M. Use the same likelihood/prior/pooling
specification and HMC budget as the primary comparison. Freeze fixtures,
starts, and seeds before scaling HMC. Run rotated mode at every size; retain
a legacy control at M=10. This is a scaling test, not a Monte Carlo recovery
ladder. Do not alter statistical complexity between sizes.

Large-fit storage may select structural and diagnostic parameters instead of
all observation-level generated quantities. Preserve a normal `stanfit`, the
selected-parameter manifest, all chain sampler diagnostics, and independent
target/gradient scaling timings. This changes stored output, not inference.
Do not construct dense N-by-N covariance matrices. Retain explicit resource
failures instead of replacing full HMC with likelihood timing and declaring
success.

## Policy and evidence comparisons

For usable primary pairs, compare reconstructed economic/posterior predictive
summaries, repeated bridge estimates, and the same merger, +10% marginal-cost
shock, and combined shock. Use the existing observed-share simulation anchor
for the rotation comparison. Latent-quality propagation is a separately
labeled substantive experiment and must not confound the coordinate test.

Fit once and reuse the posterior for all scenarios. Freeze simulation draw
indices and RNG seeds. Preserve solver failures and report bridge repeat
variability. Distinguish native density equality, which is a mathematical
implementation gate, from noisy empirical bridge estimates.

## Storage and decision

Archive large artifacts under an ignored Windows-drive directory. Only small
scripts, manifests, summary tables, and reports are candidates for Git.
The legacy default remains until both equality and HMC evidence justify a
change. Successful target tests alone are not evidence of faster sampling.
